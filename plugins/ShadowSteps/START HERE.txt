SHADOW STEPS + COMMON TONE

Hear both instruments: SD / Projects / Shadow Steps / 03 Two Track Jam.wav

SHADOW STEPS — Plugin track
Start transport. Play MIDI notes 36 through 39 for one bar, then let it loop.
For one-pad playing, use note 47: strong hits play the low drum, soft hits
play the upper sounds.
Hold 40 to shift the upper drums, 41 to hear notes in the rests, or 42 for
a pitched canon. Release to return. Tap 44 and play another bar to replace
the keeper. Tap 45 before the new bar finishes to cancel the replacement.

COMMON TONE — separate Plugin track
Play a key at MIDI 48 or higher. Hold pad note 36, 37 or 38 to hear different
chords under that melody note. While holding one, tap 42 to remember it in
A. Release the preview and tap 40 to recall A. Tap 39 to drum its rhythm.
Use 43 to store B, 41 to recall B, and 44 to drop the sustained chord.
The MPC's current pad note layout determines the physical pad positions.

01 Shadow Steps.mid and 02 Common Tone.mid are the separate performances.
03 Two Track Jam.mid contains both sets of player gestures. Assign the two
tracks to the named plugins. The WAVs are stereo listening references.
Start with empty instances when replaying the demo from the beginning.

SD / Projects / Shadow Steps / Editable variations / Canon.mid contains the
arranged drum and answer notes on separate tracks. Play the drum track on a
kit and try the answer on Pluck Bass or Wurli. The matching Original.mid,
Offbeat.mid and Holes.mid files sit beside it.
